## Research Interests

My research focuses on developing cutting-edge ML models for high-speed neural imaging. I work at the intersection of computer vision, scientific ML, and systems neuroscience to decode neural activity from multimodal, noisy datasets.

**Key Areas:**
- Deep Learning for Biomedical Imaging (CNNs, Diffusion)
- Multimodal Decoding (Calcium + Voltage)
- High-Throughput Imaging Pipelines (kHz systems)
